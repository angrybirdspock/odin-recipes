Galaxy Popcorn

Description

One of this birds favorite snacks! I know, a bird that loves corn, how original. But this cosmic coated snack is perfect for a party, or just a fun flying treat!
White chocolate coated popcorn that reminds me of flying through a frozen hydrogen astroid field. The sweet compliments the base popcorn exceptionally well. I've even heard of some variations made with a mushroom popcorn?
Original recipe found of dellcovespices.com

Ingrediants

  • 3 tablespoons canola oil
  • 1/3 cup popcorn kernels
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 12 oz. White chocolate chips
  • Purple gel food color
  • Blue gel food color
  • Silver edible glitter
  • Galaxy themed sprinkles

Directions

  1. To make the popcorn, add the canola oil and 2 to 3 of the mushroom kernels to a large pot with a lid. Heat on high until the kernels pop. That will let you know that your oil is hot enough.
  2. Add the remaining kernels and the salt. Cover, continue cooking on high, shaking the pot often, until the popping stops. Remove the popcorn to a sheet pan covered with parchment paper.
  3. Add the white chocolate chips to a bowl and place over a pot of simmering water. Make sure the bottom of the bowl does not touch the water and keep the heat low. Stir until melted.
  4. Divide the melted white chocolate into three bowls. Add a couple of drops of the blue gel color to one bowl and stir. Add another drop or two if you want the color deeper. Repeat the process with the second bowl of melted chocolate and the purple gel color.
  5. Add one drop of purple to the third bowl of melted chocolate and stir. The color should be a light lavender but add another drop if you want it darker but still not as dark as the purple bowl.
  6. Drizzle some of the blue chocolate over the popcorn. Next drizzle on some purple followed by some lavender. Repeat with the colors until you’re happy with the galaxy effect.
  7. Sprinkle with the edible glitter and top with the galaxy themed sprinkles. Allow to sit for a few minutes for the chocolate to set before serving.
